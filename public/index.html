Guitar Tuner

Click a string to hear it. Click it again to stop.

Tuning meter (microphone)

Play a string near the microphone. The meter shows the nearest string of the selected tuning and whether it is sharp or flat. Audio stays on this device.

Custom tuning editor

Enter the note for each string, lowest first, using a letter, optional # or b, and an octave number (for example E2, F#3, Bb1). Saved in this browser only.

Keyboard: keys 1-6 act like clicking strings from lowest to highest. Left and Right arrows move to the previous or next note and loop it. Esc or Space stops.

Common chords

Open shapes in standard tuning (E A D G B E). An x under the diagram means the string is not played, and o means open. Play strums the chord through your speakers.

Chord progressions

Pick a key and a progression to see its chords. Roman numerals are the chords of the key: upper case is major, lower case is minor. The key is the sound you hear, so with a capo the diagrams show the shape to finger.
